Using {{APP}}

{{APP}} helps you review and clean up the photo library on your own iPhone. You need an Apple device running a supported version of iOS and permission to access Photos. Features may differ between versions and may change as we improve the app.

Your photos and deletions

  • You decide what is deleted. Swiping to delete only stages an item in {{APP}}'s Review tray. Nothing is removed from Photos until you confirm in the tray and in iOS's own confirmation.
  • Confirmed deletions go to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album, where you can recover them for up to 30 days. After that, or after you empty Recently Deleted, they are gone permanently.
  • With iCloud Photos on, deletions also apply to iCloud, your other devices and any Shared Library.
  • Suggestions such as "duplicate", "similar", "blurry" or "best shot" are automated estimates and can be wrong. Please check before you confirm.
  • Keep a backup of photos that matter to you. We never have a copy of your photos and cannot recover them.
