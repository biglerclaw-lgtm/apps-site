Principles shared by every {{BRAND}} app

  • Your app data stays on your device. Photos, decisions, habits, journals and statistics are stored and processed on your iPhone. {{BRAND}} runs no server that receives them.
  • No accounts. You never sign up, sign in or give us your email to use an app.
  • Optional iCloud, your iCloud. Where an app offers iCloud sync, it is off until you turn it on and it uses your own private iCloud storage, which {{BRAND}} cannot access.
  • Purchases by Apple. Subscriptions, free trials and lifetime purchases are handled by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.
  • Ads only in free versions, and never with your app data. Free versions show ads through the AppLovin MAX SDK and mediated networks such as Google AdMob. Those companies may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location, device information and ad interactions. Your photos, habits, journal or other in-app content are never given to them. Buying Pro removes ads.
  • You're in control. Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and each app's "Ad Privacy Choices" setting let you refuse tracking and personalized ads; you can also change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • No analytics SDKs of our own, and we never sell your app data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California's CCPA/CPRA and similar US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Each app's policy explains how to use these rights for that app. You can always email {{EMAIL}}, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

{{BRAND}} apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

If you email us

We receive your email address and your message and use them only to reply. We keep support emails only as long as needed, normally no more than 24 months, and delete them sooner on request.
