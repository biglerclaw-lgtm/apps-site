{{APP}} Pro: subscriptions, free trial and lifetime purchase

The free version of {{APP}} includes ads and the features described in the app as free. {{APP}} Pro unlocks the Pro features and removes ads. Pro is available as:

  • An auto-renewing subscription (yearly, and any other period offered in the app). The yearly plan may include a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, as an Apple introductory offer.
  • A lifetime purchase: a one-time, non-consumable purchase. No renewals.

The price, billing period and trial length are shown in the app before you confirm and in Apple's purchase sheet. Prices vary by country and may include taxes.

How billing works

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase. If you start a free trial, you are not charged during the trial; your Apple ID is charged the subscription price when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends, at the then-current price.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or from the "Manage subscription" option in the app's Settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during a free trial, Apple may end your access to Pro right away.
  • Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription or the lifetime purchase.
  • If a price increases, Apple notifies you in advance and, where required, asks for your consent before renewing at the new price.
  • If you have a subscription and later buy the lifetime purchase, cancel the subscription in Settings so you are not charged twice. The app reminds you.
  • Purchases are shareable with up to five other family members through Apple's Family Sharing, where the app says so.

Refunds and restores

All payments are processed by Apple. We cannot see or issue refunds; request one from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple's refund decisions and your statutory rights (for example, EU/UK withdrawal rights for digital content) are handled by Apple under its terms. Use "Restore Purchases" in the app to get Pro back on a new device signed in to the same Apple ID.
