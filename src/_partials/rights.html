Your privacy rights

Because {{APP}} has no account and no server, we do not hold a profile about you. Your app data is on your device, where you can view, export (where the app offers it) and delete it yourself. For data the ad partners collect, you can use the choices above or contact the partner directly. Whatever your location, you can email us and we will help.

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Who is responsible. We, the developer of {{APP}}, are the controller for the limited personal data we handle (for example, emails you send us, and the decision to integrate ad SDKs). AppLovin, Google and other ad partners act as independent controllers for the data they collect to deliver and measure ads.
  • Legal bases. Personalized ads and the access to device identifiers they need: your consent, collected through the consent screen and Apple's tracking prompt. Showing non-personalized ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and security: the legitimate interests of the developer of {{APP}} and the ad partners in funding a free app and keeping ads safe. Replying to your support email: our legitimate interest in helping you (or steps you ask for before a contract). Purchases: handled by Apple under Apple's terms.
  • Your rights. Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection (including to processing based on legitimate interests), data portability, and withdrawing consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Withdraw ad consent in {{APP}} Settings > Ad Privacy Choices; object or ask about anything else by emailing {{SUPPORT_LINK}}.
  • Complaints. You can complain to your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to fix things first.
  • International transfers. Ad partners may process data in the United States and other countries. They rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where they are certified, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; see their policies above.

California and other US states (CCPA / CPRA and similar laws)

  • Categories collected in the last 12 months (by ad partners through the free version only): identifiers (IDFA with your permission, IDFV, SDK IDs, IP address); internet or other electronic network activity (ad impressions and taps); approximate geolocation derived from IP; device information. Inferences may be drawn by ad partners for personalized ads if you allow it. We do not collect sensitive personal information, and your in-app data is never shared with ad partners.
  • Sources and purposes. Collected directly from your device by the ad SDKs, for the advertising, measurement, security and fraud-prevention purposes described above.
  • "Sale" and "sharing". We do not sell personal information for money. Letting ad partners use identifiers for cross-context behavioral advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing" under California law. To opt out: choose "Ask App Not to Track", and/or open {{APP}} Settings > Ad Privacy Choices and opt out of sale/sharing, or email us. You can also buy Pro to remove ads entirely.
  • Your rights. To know/access, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, and limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not use any). We will not discriminate against you for using these rights; the free version stays the same whether or not you opt out. An authorized agent may make a request on your behalf with your written permission. Because we hold no account data, we may not be able to link ad-partner data to you; we will tell you which partner to contact and how.
  • Retention. See Data retention.
  • Minors. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

Residents of other US states with consumer privacy laws (for example Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon) have similar rights to access, delete, correct and opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same steps above or email us. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line.
