Purchases and subscriptions

{{APP}} Pro is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple handles the payment, your payment details, your Apple ID and your billing address. {{BRAND}} never sees your name, email address or payment information. The app only receives a signed confirmation from Apple saying which product is active (for example, "Yearly, renews on a date" or "Lifetime") so it can unlock Pro and switch off ads. That check happens on your device. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

App Store Connect gives us aggregated, non-identifying sales reports (for example, how many trials started in a country). Apple does not give us your identity.
