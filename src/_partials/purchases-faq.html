How do I restore my purchase?
  1. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy {{APP}} Pro (Settings > [your name] > Media & Purchases).
  2. Open {{APP}}, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases (it is also on the Pro screen).
  3. If you got Pro through Family Sharing, make sure Purchase Sharing is on for your family group.

Restoring needs an internet connection to reach the App Store. If it says nothing was found, email us with the approximate purchase date (never send card details); we can't see your Apple ID, but we can help you check.

How do I cancel my free trial or subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap {{APP}} Pro, then Cancel Subscription (or Cancel Free Trial).

Or, inside {{APP}}: Settings > Manage subscription opens the same Apple screen.

To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The app can send you a reminder 2 days before the trial ends if notifications are on. If you cancel during a trial, Apple may end Pro access right away. If you cancel a paid subscription, you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes every payment, so refunds come from Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose "Request a refund" and pick the {{APP}} purchase. {{BRAND}} can't issue refunds directly, but email us if something went wrong and we'll do what we can.

I have a subscription. Can I switch to Lifetime?

Yes. Buy Lifetime in the app, then cancel the subscription in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions so it doesn't renew. The app reminds you to do this. Apple decides whether any unused subscription time is refunded.

Why do I see ads, and how do I turn them off?

The free version is funded by ads. {{APP}} Pro (subscription or lifetime) removes all ads. To stop personalized ads without buying anything, choose "Ask App Not to Track" (or change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking) and review your choices in {{APP}} Settings > Ad Privacy Choices. Details are in the privacy policy.
