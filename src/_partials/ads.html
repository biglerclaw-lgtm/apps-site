Advertising in the free version

The free version of {{APP}} shows ads. Buying {{APP}} Pro (a subscription or the lifetime purchase) removes all ads, and the ad SDK then stops requesting ads.

Ads are delivered by AppLovin MAX, an ad mediation SDK built into the app. MAX may fill an ad slot from AppLovin itself or from other ad networks it mediates, such as Google AdMob. These companies, not us, receive and process the data described below. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned.

The app does not request precise location for ads and does not give ad SDKs access to your contacts, camera, microphone or Health data.

Your ad choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (all users). The first time ads might be personalized, the app explains why and then shows Apple's prompt. Choose "Ask App Not to Track" and the ad SDKs cannot use your IDFA or track you across other companies' apps and websites; you will still see ads, but they are non-personalized or based on the content of the app. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking (per app, or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" for every app).
  • In-app consent manager (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws). Where the law requires it, a consent screen (Google's User Messaging Platform, shown through AppLovin MAX) asks before any data is used for personalized ads, and lets you accept, reject or pick partners and purposes. You can reopen it at any time from {{APP}} Settings > Ad Privacy Choices to change or withdraw your consent, or to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Remove ads entirely by buying {{APP}} Pro. A subscription or lifetime purchase turns off all ads.
  • Limit ad personalization at the partner. Google: My Ad Center.
