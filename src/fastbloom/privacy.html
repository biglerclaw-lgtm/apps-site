Who we are

{{APP}} is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy covers the {{APP}} iPhone app, its widgets, Live Activities and Siri and Shortcuts actions. {{APP}} is not affiliated with any maker of GLP-1 or other medications. Questions: {{SUPPORT_LINK}}.

Your data stays on your device

What you log in {{APP}} is stored in a database in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. It is not stored in a CloudKit database. Apart from the small fast copy described in iCloud, it is not stored in iCloud or in the storage area shared with widgets:

  • your fasts: start and end times, goal length and the fasting plan used, plus any custom plans you create;
  • journal notes and the mood, energy and hunger ratings you add;
  • weight, waist, water and protein entries;
  • GLP-1 logs: medication, dose and injection site, and any side effects you record;
  • fasting-buddy snapshots you import from a share code (see Things you choose to share).

Your preferences are kept in the app's settings on the device: for example your units and reminder choices{{#ads}}, a cached note of whether Pro is active, and your "do not sell or share" choice{{/ads}}{{^ads}}, and a cached note of whether Pro is active{{/ads}}. If you answer the optional safety check during onboarding (whether you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, have a history of an eating disorder, or take diabetes medication), your answers are stored in the same on-device settings and used only to adjust which fasts and guidance the app offers you. They are never sent anywhere{{#ads}} and never given to ad SDKs{{/ads}}.

We have no server for any of this and cannot see it. Like any app's data, it is included in your own device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which Apple and you control.

iCloud: a copy of your fasts

So that you never lose a fast if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone, {{APP}} keeps a small copy of your fasts in your own iCloud key-value storage (a small settings store Apple provides to each app, tied to your Apple ID). It holds two things:

  • The fast that is running now: start time, goal length, plan name, an internal ID, an ID for the iPhone that saved it and the time it was saved.
  • Your finished fasts: for each one, its start and end time, goal length, plan name and an internal ID, plus the IDs of fasts you deleted so they are not brought back. Journal notes and mood, energy and hunger ratings are not included.
  • This copy is on by default. Switch off "Keep fast history in my iCloud" in {{APP}} Settings > Export and restore to remove the finished-fast copy from iCloud and stop updating it. "Delete everything" removes both parts.
  • No notes, weight, water, protein, GLP-1, safety-check or Apple Health data is stored in iCloud, and {{APP}} does not use a CloudKit database.
  • The copy is held by Apple in your iCloud account. We, the developer, cannot access it. If iCloud is off for {{APP}}, nothing is stored there.

On this iPhone only, {{APP}} also keeps the running fast (the same few fields) in the iOS Keychain, set to "this device only", so a fast in progress survives deleting and reinstalling the app even without iCloud. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted; "Delete everything" removes it.

Apple Health

Connecting {{APP}} to Apple Health is optional and off until you turn it on in the app. iOS then asks you which data types to allow, and you can change this any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > {{APP}}.

  • {{APP}} writes the body mass (weight), water and protein that you log in the app.
  • {{APP}} reads body mass, water and protein, so your logs from other apps and devices show up, and dietary energy (food calories). Food energy is used for one thing only: if another app logs food while a fast is running, {{APP}} asks whether you broke your fast. It is not stored as a diet log or used for anything else.
  • Health data is processed on your iPhone. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with {{#ads}}ad SDKs, {{/ads}}data brokers or any other third party.
  • {{APP}} does not read or write medications in Apple Health. GLP-1 logs are kept only inside {{APP}}.

Weekly recap: AI on your iPhone

  • The optional weekly recap is written by Apple's on-device Foundation Models, which run entirely on your iPhone. It is available on iOS 26 or later, on devices that support Apple Intelligence with Apple Intelligence turned on.
  • On other devices, {{APP}} builds the recap from a built-in template instead.
  • Either way, nothing is sent to us or to any server. There is no cloud AI service.

Widgets, Live Activities and Siri

To show widgets and handle quick actions, {{APP}} keeps a small summary in a storage area on your iPhone that is shared only with its own widgets and extensions (an App Group). It holds: the running fast's start time, goal and plan name; today's water total; counts toward your weekly goals; whether Pro is active; a local copy of the running-fast backup; and commands you trigger from a widget or Siri (such as "start fast" or "add water") waiting for the app to apply them.

  • Widgets and Live Activities can be seen on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. They show your running fast and progress, so think about who can see your phone.
  • Siri and Shortcuts actions run through Apple's App Intents on your iPhone and use the same local summary. {{APP}} does not send your requests anywhere.

Things you choose to share

{{APP}} never sends anything on its own. These features only share what you choose, through the iOS share sheet:

  • Fast receipts: an image summarizing a fast, which you can share or save to Photos.
  • Buddy share codes: a code you send to a friend so they can follow your fast. A code contains a display name you choose, a random code ID, your running fast's start time and goal, and your weekly goal progress. Nothing else. There is no server: the code travels only through the app or person you send it to, and we never see it. When you import a friend's code, that snapshot is stored on your iPhone only.

Once you share something, the app or person you send it to handles it under their own privacy practices.

Permissions

All permissions are optional, and each is asked for only when you first use the feature that needs it. {{APP}} works without them.

  • Notifications: when you reach your fasting goal, opt-in eating-window and break-fast reminders, and a reminder 2 days before a free trial turns into a paid subscription. All are scheduled locally on your iPhone. {{APP}} does not use push notifications or a notification server.
  • Apple Health (read and write): see Apple Health.
  • Photos (add only): to save a fast receipt or share card to your library. {{APP}} cannot see or read your photos.
    • {{#ads}}
  • App Tracking Transparency: asked only in the free version, after a short explanation in the app, and never before you have completed your first fast. See Advertising.
    • {{/ads}}
  • Live Activities: to show your running fast on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. You can turn them off in Settings > {{APP}}.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: actions such as starting or ending a fast, run on your iPhone.

{{APP}} does not ask for your camera, location, contacts or microphone.

Network connections

{{APP}} works offline. It connects to the internet only for Apple's App Store (prices, purchases, restores and subscription status), Apple's iCloud key-value storage for the copy of your fasts (handled by iOS, see iCloud), {{#ads}}ads in the free version, {{/ads}}and links you tap, such as helpline or source websites. Apart from that iCloud copy, fasting, logging, Apple Health and the weekly recap never use the network. You can check this yourself in iOS's App Privacy Report (Settings > Privacy & Security > App Privacy Report).

"Do not sell or share my personal information"

{{APP}} Settings > Ad Privacy Choices also has a "Do not sell or share my personal information" switch. When it is on, {{APP}} tells AppLovin, and through it the networks it mediates, not to sell or share your personal information for targeted advertising, as US state privacy laws allow. Your choice is stored on your device.

When ads start, and where they appear

  • The ad SDK starts only in the free version, and Apple's tracking prompt is never shown before you have completed your first fast of an hour or more, and never to someone who told the safety check they are under 18 (see Children). Where they apply, you see the tracking explanation, Apple's prompt and the consent screen before any personalized ads.
  • Your IDFA is used only if you choose "Allow" on Apple's tracking prompt. Otherwise ads are non-personalized.
  • Banner ads appear only on the History and Learn lists. There is at most one full-screen ad per session, only after you log a completed fast, and never before your third fast. Watching a rewarded ad (to unlock one weekly recap or 7 extra days of history) is always your choice.
  • Eating-disorder and crisis resources are always free and never shown next to ads.
  • With Pro (subscription or lifetime), the ad SDK is never started: no tracking prompt, no consent screen and no ads. If you buy Pro while the app is open, ads stop right away.
  • Versions of {{APP}} built without ad keys contain no ad SDK at all; they show no ads and no data is collected for advertising.

If you see an inappropriate ad in {{APP}}, please tell us at {{SUPPORT_LINK}}.

Health and other sensitive information

Fasting patterns, weight, medications such as GLP-1s, side effects, eating-disorder history and pregnancy are health information, which many privacy laws treat as sensitive (for example "special category" data under the GDPR and "consumer health data" under some US state laws). That is why it never leaves your control: it stays on your iPhone (only the times, goal and plan name of your fasts are also copied to your own iCloud, which we cannot access), it is not sent to us, it is not shared with {{#ads}}ad partners or {{/ads}}anyone else, and it is not used for advertising, profiling or research. We do not collect, sell or share consumer health data.

  • We never sell your data and never use your fasting, health or Apple Health information for advertising.
  • {{APP}} does not use a CloudKit database, and it does not access your camera, location, contacts or microphone.

Data retention

  • On-device data is kept until you delete it (individual entries, "Delete everything" in Settings, or deleting the app). We keep no copy.
  • The iCloud copy of your fasts is kept in your iCloud until you switch it off in Export and restore (finished fasts) or use "Delete everything" (all of it). The running-fast part is replaced by your next fast.
  • The Keychain copy of a running fast stays on this iPhone, even after the app is deleted, until the fast ends or you use "Delete everything".
  • Data in Apple Health that {{APP}} wrote stays in the Health app until you delete it there.
    • {{#ads}}
  • Ad data is retained by AppLovin, Google and other ad partners according to their policies linked above.
    • {{/ads}}
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple under Apple's policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you and keep a record of the conversation, then deleted, normally within 24 months. Ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Delete single items: fasts, notes and log entries can be deleted where they are listed.
  • Delete everything: {{APP}} Settings has a "Delete everything" option, with a confirmation. It erases your {{APP}} data and settings on this iPhone, the widget and Siri data in the App Group, the Keychain copy of a running fast, and the copy of your fasts (running and finished) in your iCloud. It can't be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes its database, settings and widget data. It does not remove the copy of your fasts in iCloud, and iOS may keep the Keychain copy of a running fast. To clear both, use "Delete everything" before deleting the app. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Apple Health: data {{APP}} wrote to Health is not removed by deleting the app. Delete it in the Health app (Health > Sharing > Apps > {{APP}}, or the data type's "Show All Data" list), and revoke access in Health settings.

Children

{{APP}} is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Fasting of 24 hours or longer is limited to adults who report none of the safety-check risk factors; if you tell the app you are under 18, it does not offer extended fasts. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.{{#ads}} If you tell the app's safety check that you are under 18, the free version shows you no ads at all: the ad SDK is not started (or is stopped if it was running), Apple's tracking prompt is never shown, and "Do not sell or share" is turned on.{{/ads}}

Security

Your data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and Data Protection encryption, and Apple Health data is protected by iOS's own Health encryption and permissions. Because we don't operate a server, there is no developer-held database of your fasting or health data that could be breached. Keep your device passcode, your Apple ID and any images you share secure.

Contact

Email {{SUPPORT_LINK}} for questions, privacy requests or complaints. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply; please don't include health details or other personal information we don't need. We aim to answer within 7 days, and privacy requests within the time the law requires (30 days under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA).
