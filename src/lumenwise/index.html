Invited to a reading plan?

Someone shared a {{APP}} reading plan with you. To join:

  1. Install {{APP}} from the App Store on your iPhone (search for "{{APP}}"). It's free to download.
  2. Open {{APP}}, go to Plans > Join with a code and enter the code from your invite.

Already have {{APP}}? Tap the {{APP}} link in your invite instead. Each person's reading stays on their own iPhone; there is no account and {{APP}} never sees who you invite.
