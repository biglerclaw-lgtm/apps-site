Who we are

{{APP}} is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy covers the {{APP}} iPhone app, its widgets and its Siri and Shortcuts actions. {{APP}} is not affiliated with any church, denomination or Bible publisher. Questions: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Your data stays on your device

What you create in {{APP}} is stored in a database in the app's own private storage on your iPhone:

  • highlights, verse notes and bookmarks;
  • the reading plans you join, your progress and daily reading logs (used to count your streak);
  • prayer requests, the updates you add to them and notes on answered prayers;
  • your Ask conversations (the questions you type and the answers shown).

Your preferences are kept in the app's settings on the device: your Bible translation, reader settings (such as font, size and theme), the faith tradition you pick for Ask, your reminder time, whether the prayer journal is locked, a cached note of whether Pro is active, timestamps the app uses to space out ads, counters for rewards earned by watching a rewarded ad, and your "do not sell or share" choice.

There is no iCloud sync in this version. Nothing syncs or leaves your iPhone except what you explicitly share or export. We have no server for this data and cannot see it. Like any app's data, it is included in your own device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which Apple and you control.

Ask: AI on your iPhone

  • Ask uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models, which run entirely on your iPhone. It is available on iOS 26 or later, on devices that support Apple Intelligence with Apple Intelligence turned on.
  • Your questions and the answers are never sent to us or to any third party. There is no cloud AI service and no proxy server, and Ask works in Airplane Mode.
  • Answers can only quote Bible verses that the app looks up in its built-in text, word for word (our "Verbatim-Verse Guarantee"). The explanations around those verses are generated by the on-device model and can be wrong; see the Terms of Use.
  • On devices without Apple Intelligence, {{APP}} shows study notes that are built into the app instead. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Your Ask history is saved on your iPhone so you can come back to it, and you can delete it at any time.

Bible texts and search

The King James Version (1769 text) and the World English Bible are built into the app, so reading works offline. Both are public domain texts from eBible.org. Searching the Bible happens on your iPhone using a local index. No network connection is used for scripture or search, and we never learn what you read or search for.

Widgets and Siri

To show widgets, {{APP}} copies a small summary into a storage area shared only with its own widgets (an App Group): the verse of the day for the next few days, the title and progress of your current reading plan, your streak count, and items for the prayer check-in widget.

  • Prayer titles appear on widgets only if you turn on "show prayer titles on widgets", which is off by default. Widgets can be seen on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, so think about who can see your phone before turning this on.
  • When you answer a prayer check-in from the interactive widget, the answer is queued in the same shared area. The app applies it to your journal the next time it opens and then clears the queue.
  • Siri and Shortcuts ("verse of the day") read only this local widget summary. Links into the app use {{APP}}'s own link scheme and stay on your device.

Things you choose to share

{{APP}} never sends anything on its own. These features only share what you choose, through iOS's share sheet or the Photos app:

  • Verse cards: an image of a verse you design, shared or saved to Photos by you.
  • Sharing a verse or your plan progress, including "read together" invites for a reading plan, sent by you to someone you choose.
  • Exports of notes, highlights or your prayer journal (PDF or text). Keep these files somewhere safe; a prayer journal export contains your personal prayers.

Once you share something, the app or person you send it to handles it under their own privacy practices.

Permissions

All permissions are optional. {{APP}} works without them.

  • Notifications: a daily reading reminder, prayer check-back reminders with "Answered" and "Still praying" buttons, and a reminder 48 hours before a free trial turns into a paid subscription. All are scheduled locally on your iPhone; no notification server is used.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode to lock the prayer journal. This is handled entirely by iOS. {{APP}} only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never receives biometric data.
  • Photos (add only): to save verse-card images to your library. {{APP}} cannot see or read your photos.
  • App Tracking Transparency: asked only in the free version when ads are switched on, after a short explanation in the app. See Advertising.
  • Background audio: lets read-aloud keep playing when you lock your phone. Read-aloud uses Apple's on-device speech voices; the text is not sent anywhere.

{{APP}} does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or Health data.

Network connections

{{APP}} works offline. It connects to the internet only for Apple's App Store (prices, purchases, restores and subscription status), for ads in the free version, and for links you tap. Scripture, search, Ask and read-aloud never use the network. You can check this yourself in iOS's App Privacy Report (Settings > Privacy & Security > App Privacy Report).

"Do not sell or share my personal information"

{{APP}} Settings > Ad Privacy Choices also has a "Do not sell or share my personal information" switch. When it is on, {{APP}} tells AppLovin, and through it the networks it mediates, not to sell or share your personal information for targeted advertising, as US state privacy laws allow. Your choice is stored on your device.

When ads start, and where they appear

  • No ad SDK starts until you have finished onboarding and, where they apply, seen the tracking explanation, Apple's tracking prompt and the consent screen. Google's ad measurement is also set to wait until then.
  • Your IDFA is used only if you choose "Allow" on Apple's tracking prompt. Otherwise ads are non-personalized.
  • Banner ads appear only on the Plans list and in Search results. There is at most one full-screen ad per session, and only after you complete a plan day. Watching a rewarded ad (for example, for extra Ask questions or to try a reading plan) is always your choice.
  • Ads never appear in the Bible reader text, the prayer journal, Ask, onboarding, the Pro screen, Today or verse cards.
  • With Pro (subscription or lifetime), the ad SDK is never started: no tracking prompt, no consent screen and no ads.
  • If ads are not switched on. Ads run only in versions of the app where ad accounts are configured. In a version without them, no ad SDK starts, no tracking prompt is shown and no data goes to any third party, and the App Store privacy label for that version says "Data Not Collected".

We ask our ad partners to block ads for gambling, dating and mature content where their controls allow it. If you see an inappropriate ad in {{APP}}, please tell us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Religious beliefs and sensitive information

What you read, highlight, pray about and ask can reveal religious or philosophical beliefs, which many privacy laws treat as sensitive. That is why it never leaves your control: it stays on your iPhone, it is not sent to us, it is not shared with ad partners or anyone else, and it is not used for advertising, profiling or research. The faith tradition you pick for Ask only changes how answers are worded on your device.

What we never do

  • No account, sign-in or email address needed.
  • No server holding your data, no cloud AI, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own. (Ad SDKs may report their own diagnostics, as described above.)
  • We never sell your data and never use your reading, notes, prayers or questions for advertising.
  • This version of {{APP}} does not use iCloud or CloudKit, Apple Health or Spotlight indexing.

Data retention

  • On-device data is kept until you delete it (individual items, deleting all data in Settings, or deleting the app). We keep no copy.
  • Ad data is retained by AppLovin, Google and other ad partners according to their policies linked above.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple under Apple's policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you and keep a record of the conversation, then deleted, normally within 24 months. Ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: you can export your prayer journal, and with Pro your notes and highlights, as PDF or text.
  • Delete single items: highlights, notes, bookmarks, prayers and Ask conversations can each be deleted where they are listed.
  • Delete everything: the Privacy section of {{APP}} Settings has an option to delete all your data, with a confirmation. It erases your {{APP}} data, scheduled reminders and widget content on this iPhone and can't be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes all its on-device data, including the widget summary. It does not cancel a subscription.
  • Ad data: use your ad choices or contact the ad partner; email us and we'll help.

Children

{{APP}} is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and Data Protection encryption, and you can lock the prayer journal with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Because we don't operate a server, there is no developer-held database of your notes, prayers or questions that could be breached. Keep your device passcode, your Apple ID and any exported files secure.

Contact

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for questions, privacy requests or complaints. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply; please don't include prayer details or other personal information we don't need. We aim to answer within 7 days, and privacy requests within the time the law requires (30 days under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA).
