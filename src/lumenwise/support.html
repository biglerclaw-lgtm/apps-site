Ask and study notes

How does Ask work? Are my questions sent anywhere?

Ask is answered by Apple's Foundation Models, an AI model that runs entirely on your iPhone. Your questions and answers are never sent to us, to Apple's servers or to any other company, and Ask works in Airplane Mode. Your Ask history is saved on your iPhone, and you can delete any conversation.

Why don't I see AI answers?

On-device answers need iOS 26 or later on an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri. The model may also need time to download after you turn it on. On other iPhones, {{APP}} shows study notes that are built into the app instead.

Can I trust the answers?

Every Bible verse quoted in an answer is looked up word for word in the app's built-in text (our "Verbatim-Verse Guarantee"), and you can tap it to read it in context. The explanation around the verses is written by an AI model and can be incomplete or wrong. It is not a pastor, priest or counselor, and it does not speak for any church. Check what you read against Scripture and talk to someone you trust about questions that matter to you.

Is there a limit on questions?

The free version includes a few questions each day, and you can choose to watch a short ad for a few more. {{APP}} Pro gives you unlimited questions with no ads.

Reading the Bible

Which translations are included? Do I need internet?

The King James Version (1769 text) and the World English Bible (a modern English translation) are built into the app, so reading and search work offline. Switch translation in {{APP}} Settings. Both texts are public domain and come from eBible.org. We can only include translations we have the rights to, so popular copyrighted translations are not available.

How does read-aloud work?

Read-aloud uses the voices built into iOS, on your iPhone, and keeps playing when you lock the screen. To get a better voice, download an enhanced voice in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.

I missed a day. Did I lose my plan?

No. Reading plans let you catch up at your own pace, restart or leave a plan whenever you like. Your streak is counted from your reading days on your iPhone.

Your data and privacy

Where are my notes and prayers stored? Can the developer see them?

On your iPhone. There is no account and no server of ours, so we can't see your highlights, notes, prayers or questions. Ads in the free version never receive this information. Full details: privacy policy.

Does {{APP}} sync with iCloud? How do I move to a new iPhone?

This version doesn't sync between devices. Your data moves to a new iPhone with an iCloud Backup, a computer backup or Apple's Quick Start transfer. You can also export your prayer journal (and, with Pro, your notes and highlights) as PDF or text to keep a copy.

How do I lock my prayer journal?

Turn on the journal lock in the Privacy section of {{APP}} Settings. Opening the journal then needs Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode, and the journal is blurred when you leave the app. iOS handles the check; {{APP}} never receives your face or fingerprint data.

Will my prayers show on my Home Screen?

Not unless you want them to. Prayer widgets show neutral text unless you turn on "show prayer titles on widgets" in the Privacy section of {{APP}} Settings. It is off by default.

How do I delete my data?
  • Single items: delete highlights, notes, bookmarks, prayers or Ask conversations where they are listed.
  • Everything: use the delete-all-data option in the Privacy section of {{APP}} Settings and confirm. This erases your data, reminders and widget content on this iPhone. It can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data (but does not cancel a subscription).
What notifications does {{APP}} send?

Only the ones you turn on: a daily reading reminder, prayer check-back reminders (answer "Answered" or "Still praying" right from the notification) and, if you start a free trial, a reminder 2 days before it ends. They are scheduled on your iPhone, not sent from a server. Turn them off in Settings > Notifications > {{APP}}.
