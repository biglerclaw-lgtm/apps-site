In crisis or in danger? Get help now

  • Emergency: call your local emergency number (911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU, 000 in Australia).
  • US and Canada: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, 24/7, free and confidential.
  • US: SAMHSA National Helpline (substance use, treatment referral): 1-800-662-4357, 24/7.
  • US: Gambling help: 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537).
  • US: Free quit-smoking and vaping coaching: 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669).
  • UK and Ireland: Samaritans: 116 123. Australia: Lifeline: 13 11 14.
  • Anywhere else: findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines by country.

{{APP}} is not a crisis service and can't respond to emergencies. {{APP}} is an independent app and is not affiliated with these services.

Health and safety

Is {{APP}} medical advice or treatment?

No. {{APP}} is a self-help and tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition and does not replace a doctor, pharmacist, counselor or treatment program. Health and recovery timelines are general information with sources shown in the app; your own experience may differ. Talk to a healthcare professional about quitting, about nicotine replacement or other medicines, and before making changes if you have a health condition or are pregnant.

I drink heavily every day. Is it safe to stop suddenly?

Stopping heavy daily drinking suddenly can be dangerous (alcohol withdrawal can cause seizures and can be life-threatening). Talk to a doctor before you stop, or call SAMHSA at 1-800-662-4357 in the US. If you have withdrawal symptoms such as shaking, confusion, hallucinations or seizures, get emergency help.

Where is the panic button?

The round button in the bottom corner of every tab opens breathing, a 10-minute urge-surfing timer, your reasons, "call my person" and crisis help. You can also use the {{APP}} widget, Siri ("I have an urge in {{APP}}"), the Action Button on supported iPhones, or with VoiceOver a two-finger double-tap. The panic tools are always free and never show ads.

I slipped. Do I lose my progress?

No. Log the slip and {{APP}} keeps your quit date, your clean days and your best run, then starts a new run. Slips are part of quitting for most people.

Your data and privacy

Where is my data stored? Can the developer see it?

On your iPhone. There is no account and no server of ours, so we can't see your habits, check-ins or journal. With iCloud sync on, a copy is kept in your own private iCloud, which we also can't access. Ads in the free version never receive this information. Full details: privacy policy.

How does iCloud sync work? How do I move to a new iPhone?

Where available, turn on iCloud sync in {{APP}} Settings on each device, signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud Drive enabled. Changes take effect the next time the app opens, and the first sync can take a few minutes.

Without iCloud sync, your data moves to a new iPhone with an iCloud Backup or computer backup, or you can use Me > Settings > Your data > Export on the old phone and "Restore from a {{APP}} export" on the new one. Restoring adds what's missing and never overwrites what's there.

How do I lock {{APP}} with Face ID?

Me > Settings > Privacy & security > App lock. If Face ID fails, you can use your iPhone passcode. While locked, the panic button still works in a private mode that hides personal content. You can also hide {{APP}} in the app switcher.

What does my accountability partner see?

Only what's in the code you send: your streak numbers, plus an "I'm struggling" note or what you're quitting only if you include them. Never your journal. Codes travel through Messages or whatever app you choose; {{APP}} has no server.

How do I delete my data?
  • Single items: delete individual journal entries, slips or check-ins where they are listed, or delete a habit in its editor.
  • Everything: Me > Settings > Your data > Delete everything, then type DELETE. This erases your data, reminders and widget content on this device, and from iCloud too if sync is on. It can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
  • Deleting the app also removes its on-device data (but does not cancel a subscription).
