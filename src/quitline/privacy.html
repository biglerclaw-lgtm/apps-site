Who we are

{{APP}} is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy covers the {{APP}} iPhone app, its widgets, Live Activities and Siri/Shortcuts actions. {{APP}} is an independent self-help app and is not affiliated with any government or public "quitline" service. Questions: {{SUPPORT_LINK}}.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter in {{APP}} is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone:

  • the habits you're quitting or cutting down, quit dates, cost and amount per day, and triggers;
  • slips, urges, puff counts, taper plans, milestones and pledges;
  • daily check-ins (mood, cravings, confidence) and journal entries;
  • your reasons, including any photos you attach (copied into the app from the photo picker; {{APP}} does not get access to the rest of your library);
  • a support person's name and phone number, if you add one;
  • lesson progress and settings (reminder times, app lock, theme).

We have no server for this data and cannot see it. Patterns such as your hardest hours are calculated on your iPhone. It is included in your own device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which Apple and you control.

No iCloud sync

This version of {{APP}} has no iCloud sync: it does not use iCloud or CloudKit, and your data is not kept in step across devices. The only copies are the one on your iPhone, your own device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, which Apple and you control), and any export file you make. To move to a new iPhone, restore a backup or use Export on the old phone and "Restore from a {{APP}} export" on the new one. If a future version adds optional iCloud sync, we will describe it here and update the changelog before that version is released.

Things you choose to share

{{APP}} never sends anything on its own. These features only share what you choose, through iOS's own share sheet, Messages or Phone app:

  • Partner codes: a short code with your streak numbers (and an "I'm struggling" note or habit name only if you include them), sent by you to someone you choose. It never includes your journal.
  • Share cards: an image of a milestone you choose to share (habit name hidden by default).
  • Call or text my person and crisis lines: open the Phone or Messages app, or a helpline website. Those services have their own privacy practices.
  • Export: a file of all your data, saved or sent wherever you choose. Keep it somewhere safe; it contains your personal journal.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): check-in reminders, milestones and, if you turn them on, heads-ups before your typical high-risk times. All are scheduled locally on your iPhone; no notification server is used.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / passcode (optional app lock): handled entirely by iOS. {{APP}} only learns whether unlocking succeeded; it never receives biometric data.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick for your reasons, through Apple's photo picker.
    • {{#ads}}
  • App Tracking Transparency (free version): see Advertising.
    • {{/ads}}

Widgets and Live Activities show neutral text such as "Day 42", never what you're quitting, and you can hide {{APP}}'s content in the app switcher.

Network connections

{{APP}} works offline. It connects to the internet only for: Apple's App Store (prices, purchases, restores, subscription status); {{^ads}}and {{/ads}}links you tap, such as helpline websites{{#ads}}; and ads in the free version{{/ads}}. You can check this yourself in iOS's App Privacy Report (Settings > Privacy & Security > App Privacy Report).

Health information

Information about smoking, vaping, drinking, gambling, mood and cravings can be sensitive. That is why it never leaves your control: it stays on your device, it is not shared with {{#ads}}ad partners or {{/ads}}anyone else, and it is not used for advertising, profiling or research. {{APP}} does not read from or write to Apple Health. We do not collect "consumer health data" as defined by laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act.

What we never do

  • No account, sign-in or email address needed.
  • {{#ads}}No server holding your journey, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own. (Ad SDKs may report their own diagnostics, as described above.){{/ads}}{{^ads}}No server holding your journey, no ads, and no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.{{/ads}}
  • We never sell your data and never use your habit or journal data for advertising.

Data retention

  • On-device data is kept until you delete it (individual entries, Delete everything, or deleting the app). We keep no copy.
    • {{#ads}}
  • Ad data is retained by AppLovin, Google and other ad partners according to their policies linked above.
    • {{/ads}}
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple under Apple's policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you and keep a record of the conversation, then deleted, normally within 24 months. Ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: {{APP}} Me > Settings > Your data > Export creates a file with everything you've entered.
  • Delete everything: Me > Settings > Your data > Delete everything (type DELETE to confirm). This erases all your {{APP}} data, scheduled reminders and widget content on this device. Copies in your own device backups and export files are yours to delete.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device data. It does not cancel a subscription.
    • {{#ads}}
  • Ad data: use your ad choices or contact the ad partner; email us and we'll help.
    • {{/ads}}

Children and teens

{{APP}} is meant for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it. If you are a teen, we encourage you to also talk to a trusted adult, doctor or helpline about quitting; resources are on our support page.

Security

Your data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and Data Protection encryption, and you can add Face ID or passcode lock in {{APP}}. Because we don't operate a server, there is no developer-held database of your journey that could be breached. Keep your device passcode, your Apple ID and any exported files secure.

Contact

Email {{SUPPORT_LINK}} for questions, privacy requests or complaints. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply; please don't include more health details than you need to. We aim to answer within 7 days, and privacy requests within the time the law requires (30 days under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA).
